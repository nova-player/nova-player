Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Nova Player (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) handles information when you browse music, stream tracks, manage playlists, and keep songs in Offline Space.

01Scope of this policy

Nova Player is an Android music application that lets you discover and search songs online, stream playback, build playlists and favorites, import playlists from supported platforms, and save tracks for offline listening.

You can use the App’s core features without creating an account. By continuing to use Nova Player, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. For rules about using the App itself, please also read our Terms of Service.

02What we process

We collect only what we need to operate and improve Nova Player. Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Technical information — such as device type, operating system version, app version, language settings, and basic performance or crash indicators.
  • Analytics events — aggregated usage events in release builds (for example through Firebase Analytics) so we can understand stability and how features are used.
  • Request metadata — when online features are used, the network details required to complete search, streaming, lyrics, configuration, or playlist-import requests.
  • Feedback you send — the message content and limited device context you include when contacting us through in-app feedback.
We do not require payment card details, contacts, precise location, camera access, or microphone access for core playback.

03What stays on your device

Playlists, favorites, playback preferences, download records, and Offline Space audio or cover files are stored primarily on your device. We do not upload your downloaded music library to our servers.

  • Deleting items in the App, clearing app data, or uninstalling Nova Player removes local copies under normal operating-system behavior.
  • You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to store and play any offline content.

04Online discovery & imports

Features such as searching, browsing, streaming, fetching lyrics, loading remote configuration, or importing a playlist link (for example from supported platforms such as YouTube or Spotify) require network requests. Those requests may include search queries, track or playlist identifiers, and the URLs you submit for import.

Third-party platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Nova Player does not control their catalogs, availability, or practices.

05Service partners

We may use a small number of trusted providers to help deliver the App:

  • Analytics and diagnostics — to improve reliability and understand feature adoption.
  • Music and configuration APIs — to return search results, playback links, lyrics, and remote settings.

Partners receive only the information needed to perform their role and handle it under their own privacy statements.

06Security & retention

Where applicable, we protect data in transit using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Local listening data remains on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against every risk.

Analytics records or server logs, if created, are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

07Children

Nova Player is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

08Your choices

  • Delete offline downloads, playlists, or favorites within the App.
  • Stop further processing by uninstalling Nova Player and clearing app data.
  • Email us with privacy questions or requests where applicable law provides additional rights.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
